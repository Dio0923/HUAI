# HUAI Check design constraints

HUAI is a decision journal. The user does the thinking; the interface must make that sequence obvious, not feel like a chat or dashboard.

## Visual system

- **Paper** `#f6f3e9`, **ink** `#242e29`, **deep olive** `#293f35`, **clay** `#9e4936`, **rule** `#c9cec0`, **field** `#fffefa`. Use solid surfaces and maintain readable contrast.
- Headlines: Georgia / Times New Roman system serif. Body and controls: Arial / Helvetica system sans-serif. No downloaded fonts.
- Use a four-pixel spacing rhythm, fine rules, generous left-aligned text, and minimum 44px touch targets. Primary actions are solid deep olive with light text.
- The home screen is asymmetric: a decision prompt, a quiet editorial note, then two functional rows for the review queue and calibration. No fabricated sample records.
- At 360px, stack content in reading order without horizontal page scrolling. Keep focus indicators visible and tables readable.

## Not in this design

No gradients, translucent glass, drop shadows, decorative motion, emoji, stock or AI-generated imagery, testimonial placeholders, generic three-card feature rows, fake metrics, or added third-party fonts. Use real product imagery only when supplied and useful. No analytics or tracking scripts. Do not add design-audit tooling or a test suite to V1.

## Product voice

Short, plain English. The user commits before critique and writes their own final decision. Never recommend or imply that HUAI makes a decision for them. Preserve the safety and child-consent launch limitations in the existing product brief.
